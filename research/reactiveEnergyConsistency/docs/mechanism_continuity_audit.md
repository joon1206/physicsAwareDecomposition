For each switch, Delta h_r=sum nu_ir Delta h_i and Delta ln K=−sum nu_ir Delta g_i/(Ru Tc). Kp/Kc have the same multiplicative jump at a fixed temperature. For fixed composition, Delta ha=sum Yi Delta h_i/W_i, and Delta ea=Delta ha because R(Y)Tc is continuous. Mixture examples use the actual unburned and radical mixtures and an explicitly artificial equal-mole mixture.

The HS control leaves Cp unchanged. CpHS additionally shifts its high-branch constant coefficient. Both preserve low/reference coefficients exactly; serialized h jumps are below 2.8e-5 J/kmol. These are internally continuous to reported serialization precision, not exact-real arithmetic or independently validated source-data fits. Cp positivity extrema were computed with polynomial roots; no interval-certified positivity theorem is asserted for every imported species.
