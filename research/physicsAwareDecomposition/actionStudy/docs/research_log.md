LimitedLinear continuations nevertheless diverge strongly: up to 321 K over 80 steps. Tightening linear tolerances reduces but does not resolve the difference; extra thermodynamic Newton corrections also fail. Tightening seulex to absTol 1e-16/relTol 1e-8 exceeds its 10000-step integration cap. Rosenbrock34 alone does not resolve the partition sensitivity. Upwind with strict linear convergence passes the predefined field-scale gate (T Linf 0.000115 K in the diagnostic continuation). The complete failed runs remain under rejected_oracle_limitedLinear and refinement directories. Final experiments use the same upwind scheme and Rosenbrock34 integrator for every action; this is a first-order controlled benchmark and does not establish accuracy for a resolved physical flame. Histories will be regenerated under the final settings before benchmarking.
